Questions, answered

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. One active service, a full profile, contacts and reviews — with no time limit and no card.

What do I need to get verified?

Your NIE or CIF and proof that you work legally in Spain (autónomo registration or a company document). We check it within 24 hours.

Do clients pay through UAHUB?

No. Clients pay you directly, on the spot. We don't take a commission on bookings.

How do Boost and Featured differ?

Boost lifts one service to the top of the search list for 3 days. Featured puts your card on the home page of your city for 7 days. You can buy either on any plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid period, then your profile moves to Free — nothing gets deleted.
